Privacy Policy

Babita Classes

Last updated: 23 August 2026.

Babita Classes ("we", "us", "our") is a free, non-profit educational organization based in Kanpur, Uttar Pradesh. This policy explains what information we collect through our website and admission process, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using our website or enrolling a student with us, you agree to the practices described here, alongside our Terms and Conditions.

1. Information We Collect

  • Website visitors: You can browse our website, blog, and results page without creating an account or signing in. No personal information is required just to view the site.
  • Admission and contact forms: If you submit our Admission Form or Contact Form (hosted on Google Forms), we collect what you enter — typically a student's or guardian's name, contact number, and message. See Google's own privacy policy for how Google Forms handles submissions in transit.
  • Student records: Once a student is admitted, we maintain internal records of their name, roll number, daily attendance, and examination marks, for the purpose of running classes and publishing results.
  • AI assistant conversations: If you use the "Ask Babita Classes Assistant" chat feature on our site, the message you type is sent to Google's Gemini AI service to generate a reply. We don't attach your name or identity to this — it's a one-off message exchange, not a stored conversation history tied to you.
  • Basic visit statistics: Our website uses a third-party visitor counter (freevisitorcounters.com) to show a simple total-visits count. This may record standard technical details like your approximate visit time — it is not used by us to identify individuals.

2. How We Use Information

  • To process admissions and respond to enquiries.
  • To take attendance, record and publish examination results, and manage day-to-day class administration.
  • To communicate with students and parents/guardians about classes, notices, or changes to our terms.
  • To answer general questions through our AI assistant.

We do not sell, rent, or trade personal information to any third party.

3. Where Your Data Is Stored

Student records (names, roll numbers, attendance, and results) are stored on Google Firebase, a cloud database service, and are only accessible through a password-protected administrative panel operated by our team. Data in transit to and from our website is encrypted (HTTPS). As with any online system, no method of storage or transmission is 100% secure, but we take reasonable, industry-standard precautions to protect the records we hold.

4. Third-Party Services We Use

  • Google Firebase — hosts our database and secures our admin login.
  • Google Gemini AI — powers the optional AI assistant chat feature; messages sent through it are processed under Google's own terms.
  • Google Forms — used for our Admission and Contact forms.
  • Facebook, Instagram, YouTube, WhatsApp — linked for social updates and communication; each operates under its own privacy policy once you leave our site.
  • freevisitorcounters.com — displays a public total-visits counter on our homepage.

We encourage you to review these third parties' own privacy policies, as we do not control how they separately handle data once you interact with them directly.

5. Children's Privacy

Many of our students are minors. As stated in our Terms and Conditions, we require prior written consent from a parent or guardian before admitting any student, and student records are collected and used strictly for class administration — attendance and result-keeping — not for any commercial or marketing purpose. Parents or guardians may contact us at any time to ask what information we hold about their child, or to request a correction.

6. Data Retention

If a student leaves or is removed from our active list, we retain their historical attendance and result records rather than deleting them outright, so that past marksheets and attendance history remain accurate and available if ever needed. Records are removed from active day-to-day lists but kept for legitimate record-keeping purposes.

7. Cookies & Local Storage

Our website stores a small, non-personal preference — whether you've chosen dark mode — in your browser's local storage, purely so the site remembers your display preference on return visits. This is never sent to us or to any third party. The visitor-counter service mentioned above may set its own cookie or tracking pixel independently of us.

8. Your Rights & Choices

  • You may ask us what personal or student data we hold about you or your child.
  • You may request a correction if any information is inaccurate.
  • You may request removal from our active records at any time (subject to our record-retention practice described above for historical academic records).

To exercise any of these, please reach out through the contact details below.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in how we operate our website or handle data. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. Continued use of our website after changes are posted means you accept the revised policy.

10. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please reach out to us using the details in the footer below, or via our Contact Form.
