Admission and Enrollment

  1. Admission Process: To take admission, students can either fill out the offline form available at our office or submit the online form on our website.
  2. Eligibility Criteria: Our organization is located in Kanpur, and only students residing in the area will be eligible for admission.
  3. Age Requirement: Students must be at least 5 years old and able to speak properly to be eligible for admission. Children under 5 years old are not allowed.
  4. Prior Consent: We require prior written consent from parents/guardians for students to be admitted to our organization.

Student Behavior and Conduct

  1. Attendance: If a student is absent for more than 30 days (excluding Sundays and public holidays) without prior application, they will be removed from our student list and will need to reapply for admission.
  2. Misconduct and Cheating: We reserve the right to suspend or expel students who engage in misconduct, cheating, or destruction of our property. "Misconduct" refers to behavior that is detrimental to the learning environment, including but not limited to bullying, harassment, or destruction of property.

  4. Academic Integrity: Babita Classes expects its students to maintain the highest standards of academic integrity. Any instances of plagiarism, cheating, or other forms of academic dishonesty will be dealt with strictly and may result in penalties or disciplinary action.
